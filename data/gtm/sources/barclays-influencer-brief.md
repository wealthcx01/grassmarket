# Getting to the owners of Barclays Live — influence map

**Prepared 13 July 2026 · Sources: LSEG/I-B-E-S contributor records (Barclays = contributor 10333) sampled across 21 US & European TMT large caps, plus web research (Barclays press releases, trade press)**

## Who actually owns Barclays Live

Barclays Live is the investment bank's **institutional research & analytics portal** (web + iOS/Android), relaunched in February 2025 as a "next-generation client research portal." It is owned by **Barclays Research** — not the Markets eTrading organisation. The ownership chain is short:

**Brad Rogoff — Global Head of Research** (since 1 Oct 2024) is the platform's owner; he was the only executive quoted at the relaunch. He reports to **Stephen Dainton, President of Barclays Bank PLC & Head of Investment Bank Management**. No individual Barclays Live product lead is publicly named (the 2018-era Markets digital executives have all departed), so **Research leadership is the door** — there is no separate product-owner side entrance visible from outside.

The key intermediate hop is **Rob Rouse, Head of Americas Equity Research** — the line manager of most of the analysts below, one step from each of them and one step from Rogoff. **Jeff Meli** (Rogoff's predecessor, still a prominent Barclays research voice) is an alternative senior door-opener, though his exact current title couldn't be verified.

## The influencers (from LSEG contributor data)

Every one of these people publishes *through* Barclays Live daily — the platform is their distribution channel — so any of them can credibly route a conversation about retail/HNW reach of Barclays research to the people who run it. Full contact detail is in the spreadsheet; the ranking logic:

**Tier 1 — senior franchise names with retail-heavy coverage (New York/SF):**
**Kannan Venkateshwar** (media & telecom: NFLX, DIS, T, VZ, TMUS — the widest footprint in our sample), **Ross Sandler** (internet: GOOGL, META, AMZN, UBER — the retail-favorite megacaps, high media profile), and **Tom O'Malley** (semis: NVDA, AVGO, INTC, QCOM — NVDA being the most retail-traded stock on the street). For these three, retail participation in their covered names is already a research theme, which makes your opening conversation natural rather than a favor.

**Tier 2 — established US seniors:** **Tim Long** (AAPL, CSCO) and **Raimo Lenschow** (MSFT, CRM, ORCL), with **Saket Kalia** (ADBE) as a same-team alternate.

**Tier 3 — London seats, physically closest to the platform organisation:** **Mathieu Robilliard** (EU telecom/media; on Barclays' 2025 Extel Europe roster), **Maurice Patrick** (VOD), **Simon Coles** (ASML, ERICb), **Sven Merkt** (SAP). If the conversation is better had in London — where Research management and much of the IB sit — these are the entry points.

## Suggested path

1. Open with a Tier-1 analyst whose coverage overlaps your thesis (retail flow into their BUY-rated names is the hook — it flatters their franchise and is data you can bring).
2. Ask for the internal route to **Rob Rouse** (their boss), framed as "who owns how Barclays research reaches non-institutional audiences."
3. Rouse → **Brad Rogoff**, the platform owner. Dainton is the level above if a sponsor-level conversation is ever needed.

## Caveats

- Contact details are I/B/E/S contributor records — accurate for research staff, but note communications to sell-side research are typically logged/compliance-monitored; a warm referral beats a cold email here.
- The sample is 21 TMT tickers; the same method reconstructs any other Barclays sector team (or any other bank) on request.
- Titles in I/B/E/S ("Equity Analyst") understate seniority — several of these are Managing Directors.
- Unverified items are flagged in the Target Owners tab (Meli's current title; absence of a named Live product lead).
