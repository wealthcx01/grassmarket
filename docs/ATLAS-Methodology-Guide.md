## Why it was retired rather than updated

This guide was an informative companion pinned to **Methodology v1.1**, and it had drifted:

- it predated **v1.4** (the four-index composite), **v1.5** (the critical-control cap) and **v1.6**
  (the founder review gate);
- its governance section described the **Rating Committee** as the operative sign-off, which
  ADR-0041 made dormant;
- it did not mention the C-index, the critical-control cap, or the one-number rule (ADR-0040).

Its audience — a client's CTO, an acquirer's diligence team, a prospective panel expert — is exactly
the white paper's audience. Keeping both would mean maintaining two documents for one reader, and
this file is the evidence of what happens when one of them slips: **two documents claiming different
normative versions is worse than either choice**.

So it was folded in, not re-baselined. The formal model, the behavioural properties, the elicitation
protocol, the reliability programme, the value bridge and the prior-framework comparison all survive
in the white paper, updated to v1.6 and extended with a validation-evidence section and a limitations
register that names the coefficient status plainly.

**This stub is kept rather than deleted** so that existing links resolve to an explanation instead of
a 404.
